Each quest: talk to the chapter's mentor, travel to the encounter, defeat that chapter's antagonists (with the chapter's allies fighting beside you) or train. Arc finales grant rank, skill points and the story items (Ogre Street Gloves, Ripple Breathing Mask, Stone Mask, Pillar remains, Cybernetic Overhaul, Red Stone of Aja).
