## Unique visuals

Each genjutsu has its own `illusion` profile: a vision pattern picked from its own description (title words first), colours from the colour words it uses, and per-technique element count, speed, density and particle. A uniqueness pass guarantees no two techniques share pattern + colours + count. The victim sees the vision full-screen; everyone sees the matching world effect.
